## 3. Audit of Case 2 Minimum-Element-Size Warning (21 Clamped Elements)

### Context & Diagnostic Output
During the execution of Abaqus CAE `adaptiveRemesh` for Case 2, the replay journal `models/pandey_kumar_mode1/259_f1809_multistate_recovery/case2/case_2_abaqus.rpy` logged:
```
#: Warning: The minimum element size constraint was enforced for 21 element sizes generated by variable MISESERI of remeshing rule Rule_MethodA_case_2_p1.
```

### Numerical & Spatial Investigation
A dedicated finite element geometry audit (`scripts/remeshing/audit_f1809_case2_clamped_elements.py`) was executed on the 118,640-element mesh `PK_F1809_CASE_2_ADAPT_PHYS.inp`:

1. **Relation of Constraint to Phase-Field Scale:**
   - The configured `min_element_size_mm = 0.0015 mm` ($1.5\,\mu\text{m}$).
   - The phase-field regularizing length scale is $\ell_0 = 0.0075\,\text{mm}$ ($7.5\,\mu\text{m}$).
   - The critical fine-resolution requirement is $h \le \ell_0/2 = 0.00375\,\text{mm}$ ($3.75\,\mu\text{m}$).
   - Therefore, the configured minimum size is $1.5\,\mu\text{m} = \ell_0/5$, which is **$2.5\times$ finer than the required $\ell_0/2$ threshold**.

2. **Spatial Distribution of the Clamped Elements:**
   - The 21 elements where the Zienkiewicz–Zhu error formula requested $h < 1.5\,\mu\text{m}$ occur in regions of extreme geometric singularity and stress concentration in the multi-state envelope donor.
   - Specifically, 20 of the top 30 smallest elements in the entire mesh are located around the re-entrant slit notch tip ($x \approx 0.50\,\text{mm}$) and the circular hole boundary ($x \in [0.55, 0.75]\,\text{mm}$).
   - Only 1 of the top 30 smallest elements (EID 505 at $x = 0.94959, y = 0.58419$, $h = 0.614\,\mu\text{m}$) lies within the outer ligament domain.

3. **Outer Ligament Resolution Invariance:**
   - In the combined outer ligament domain ($x \in [0.90, 1.00], y \in [0.55, 0.75]\,\text{mm}$), exactly **6,138 elements** are present.
   - The smallest element has $h = 0.614\,\mu\text{m}$ (a boundary-conforming triangle).
   - The largest element in the entire outer ligament has $h = 2.713\,\mu\text{m}$ (threshold is $\ell_0/2 = 3.750\,\mu\text{m}$).
   - Exactly **6,138 out of 6,138 elements (100.00%)** strictly satisfy $h \le \ell_0/2$.

4. **Conclusion:**
   Enforcing the `0.0015 mm` minimum size constraint clamps sub-micron requests to $1.5\,\mu\text{m}$. Because $1.5\,\mu\text{m} \ll 3.75\,\mu\text{m}$, clamping preserves the condition $h \le \ell_0/2$ unconditionally. The warning represents normal, healthy operation of the sizing bound and has **zero adverse impact** on the $100.00\%$ outer ligament coverage claim.
