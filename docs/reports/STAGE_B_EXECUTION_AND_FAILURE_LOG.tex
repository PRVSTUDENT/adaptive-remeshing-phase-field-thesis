The recovery separated solver validity from CAE/postprocessing failures.  H0,
H1, and H2-PUB have accepted serial solver results, and consolidated CAE job
\texttt{1376236} provides the RF2--U2 data used by the committed
h-convergence analysis.  H1 and H2-PUB recovery jobs were first executions,
not retries of a failed solver.  H0 was not rerun.

digitization rather than exact author data.

because the consolidated CAE contour export failed.

universally supervisor-approved.

thread, MPI, hybrid, or GPU scaling.
\end{itemize}
