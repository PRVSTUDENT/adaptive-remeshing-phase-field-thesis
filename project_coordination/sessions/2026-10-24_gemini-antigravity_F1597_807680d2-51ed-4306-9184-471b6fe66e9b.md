## 2. History-Ratcheting Defect Resolution

### A. Root-Cause Defect in Turn 117
In Turn 117, intermediate Newton-Raphson trial displacement corrections caused strain spikes inside equilibrium iterations. In the previous implementation:
```fortran
HIST = SV_H_TRIAL(PHYSIDX, KPT)
IF (POS_M .GT. HIST) THEN
  HIST = POS_M
  SV_H_TRIAL(PHYSIDX, KPT) = POS_M
ENDIF
```
Because `SV_H_TRIAL` in the common block was never reset between internal Newton iterations of an increment, intermediate overshoots permanently ratcheted `SV_H_TRIAL` upward. This created an artificial positive feedback loop ($H \uparrow \to d \uparrow \to \text{stiffness} \downarrow \to \text{strain} \uparrow \to H \uparrow$) that drove premature specimen parting down to $1.11\,\mathrm{N}$ and $d = 1.001$.

### B. Repaired Non-Ratcheting Implementation
The candidate driving history is now evaluated directly against the **committed** state from the previous increment and the current trial strain:
```fortran
C NON-RATCHETING DRIVING ENERGY EVALUATION:
C Evaluate candidate history against committed state without iteration overshoots
IF (POS_M .GT. SV_H_COMMITTED(PHYSIDX, KPT)) THEN
  HIST = POS_M
ELSE
  HIST = SV_H_COMMITTED(PHYSIDX, KPT)
ENDIF
SV_H_TRIAL(PHYSIDX, KPT) = HIST
```
1. Intermediate trial overshoots in iteration $k$ do **not** contaminate iteration $k+1$.
2. Candidate driving history $H$ reflects the current trial displacement field $u^{(k)}$.
3. Upon increment acceptance by Abaqus (`UEXTERNALDB, LOP=2`), the converged `SV_H_TRIAL` is committed to `SV_H_COMMITTED`.
4. Upon cutback / retry (`UEXTERNALDB, LOP=1`), `SV_H_TRIAL` is cleanly restored from `SV_H_COMMITTED`.
