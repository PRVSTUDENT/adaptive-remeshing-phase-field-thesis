### Verified Root Cause
1. **2D Displacement Representation**: In Abaqus/Standard 2D solid models (e.g. plane strain CPE4 or 2D continuum coordinates), the nodal field output `U` in the `.odb` database represents the 2D displacement vector $(u_1, u_2)$ with `componentLabels = ['U1', 'U2']` and `len(v.data) == 2`.
2. **Missing U3 in 2D ODB**: Abaqus field output `U` never exports a third displacement component for planar continuum meshes, even when user elements (UEL) active on degree of freedom 3 exist in the model.
3. **Flawed Assumption**: The previous extractor and continuation driver incorrectly assumed that `len(v.data) == 3` on the mechanical ODB and that `v.data[2]` represented the phase field damage $d$.
