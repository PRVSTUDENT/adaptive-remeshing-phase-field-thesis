## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Findings

Turn 133 investigated the energetic and mechanical discrepancy in Candidate C3 multi-increment continuation (Package 108 Revision 6, Job `1417256.mmaster02`), reconstructing the complete 16-step trajectory and localizing the first departure point and governing physical/numerical mechanism:

1. **Reconstruction & Localization of the First Departure**:
   - In Step 1 (`Stage-A`), state reconstruction is exact: $E_{\text{frac}} = 0.061016\,\mathrm{mJ}$ ($-0.002\%$ vs donor C2 $0.061017\,\mathrm{mJ}$) and $RF_2 = 184.852\,\mathrm{N}$. However, because node lower bounds in `d_lower_bounds.csv` are enforced via penalty compliance ($\Gamma_{\text{pen}} = 10.0$), initial peak damage relaxed slightly to $d_{\max} = 0.639666$ (vs donor benchmark $0.654995$).
   - The first departure from the Turn 107 benchmark occurs at **Step 3 (`Inc1-Phase1`)**: holding the Reference Point at $u_2 = 0.001966107\,\mathrm{mm}$ (+1 nm) while releasing the phase field caused $d_{\max}$ to jump from $0.639666 \to 0.655215$, surging fracture energy by **$+3.963\,\mu\mathrm{J}$ in a single 1 nm increment**. This single step's dissipation exceeded the entire 5 nm Turn 107 benchmark dissipation ($+1.388\,\mu\mathrm{J}$).

2. **The Strain-Ratcheting Positive Feedback Loop in Alternating Minimization**:
   - Telemetry audit revealed that $H_{\max}$ escalated to **$7.847\,\mathrm{MPa}$** across R6's 5 increments, whereas Turn 107 monolithic forward continuation reached only **$5.241\,\mathrm{MPa}$**.
   - Analysis of UEL lines 622-633 and Abaqus step boundary conditions uncovered the governing positive feedback mechanism:
     1. During each mechanical re-equilibration step (`Inc*-Mech2`), the material has softened locally ($g(d) = (1-d)^2$ decreased).
     2. Under displacement-controlled boundary conditions, to satisfy equilibrium with the unsoftened surrounding elastic bulk, the local displacement gradients inside the softened crack-tip elements must open wider, increasing strain $\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$.
     3. In the UEL, tensile driving energy `POS_M` is evaluated using the **undegraded** elasticity tensor $\mathbf{C}_0$:
        $$\psi_0^+(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}) = \frac{1}{2} \lambda \langle \mathrm{tr}(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}) \rangle_+^2 + \mu \boldsymbol{\varepsilon} : \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$$
     4. Because $\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$ increased, `POS_M` surges dramatically at every `Mech2` step:
        * Step 2 $\to$ 4: $5.215 \to 5.601\,\mathrm{MPa}$ ($+0.386\,\mathrm{MPa}$)
        * Step 5 $\to$ 7: $5.607 \to 6.060\,\mathrm{MPa}$ ($+0.453\,\mathrm{MPa}$)
        * Step 8 $\to$ 10: $6.066 \to 6.580\,\mathrm{MPa}$ ($+0.514\,\mathrm{MPa}$)
        * Step 11 $\to$ 13: $6.586 \to 7.169\,\mathrm{MPa}$ ($+0.583\,\mathrm{MPa}$)
        * Step 14 $\to$ 16: $7.177 \to 7.847\,\mathrm{MPa}$ ($+0.670\,\mathrm{MPa}$)
     5. This inflated `POS_M` is committed to `SV_H_COMMITTED` at the end of each `Mech2` step ($H = \max(H_{\text{old}}, \psi_0^+)$).
     6. In the subsequent phase relaxation step (`Inc*-Phase1`), this inflated history acts as an artificially amplified driving force ($2(1-d)H$), driving $d$ higher.
     7. Higher $d$ further softens the element, forcing even wider crack opening in the next mechanical step, ratcheting strains and driving energy ever higher.

3. **Disproving the Load-Step Refinement Hypothesis**:
   - Turn 132 hypothesized that sub-incrementing load (5x1 nm vs 1x5 nm) would reduce the energy departure observed in R4 ($+7.98\%$).
   - The diagnostic proves the opposite: because each staggered sub-cycle executes another mechanical re-equilibration followed by phase relaxation, sub-incrementing multiplies the number of ratcheting cycles:
     * **R4 (1 jump of 5 nm, 2 AM cycles)**: ratcheting executed twice $\to E_{\text{frac}} = 0.067386\,\mathrm{mJ}$ (+7.98% error).
     * **R6 (5 increments of 1 nm, 5 AM cycles)**: ratcheting executed five times $\to E_{\text{frac}} = 0.071527\,\mathrm{mJ}$ (+14.62% error).
   - In contrast, in monolithic coupled continuation (Turn 107 benchmark), displacement and damage advance simultaneously against the consistent degraded tangent stiffness, producing $\Delta H = +0.024\,\mathrm{MPa}$ across 5 nm with zero ratcheting.

4. **Thermodynamic Consistency**:
   - In all relaxation and re-equilibration intervals across R6 where external work is zero ($\Delta W_{\text{ext}} = 0$), total system energy change is strictly negative: $\Delta E_{\text{total}} \le 0$ (verified in test suite). The excessive dissipation is an artifact of the staggered operator split on unsoftened driving energy, not an internal energy leak or non-conservative formulation error.
