## 2. Root Cause Analysis & Mathematical Proof of Active-Set Chattering

  $$\text{If } U(I) < D_{\text{lower}}(I) \implies \mathrm{RHS} \mathrel{+}= \gamma (D_{\text{lower}} - U), \quad \mathrm{AMATRX} \mathrel{+}= \gamma \quad (\gamma = 1.0 \times 10^8\,\mathrm{kN})$$
  $$\text{If } U(I) \ge D_{\text{lower}}(I) \implies \text{penalty } = 0$$
- **Extreme Condition Mismatch**:
  The physical phase-field stiffness of an element is $K_{\phi} \approx \frac{G_c}{l_0} A \approx 9.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kN} = 0.009\,\mathrm{N}$.
  Adding $\gamma = 10^8\,\mathrm{kN}$ introduced a stiffness ratio of $1.11 \times 10^{13}$ (13 orders of magnitude), destroying double-precision floating-point accuracy and creating huge Newton overshoots.
- **2-Cycle Undamped Limit Cycle**:

On the subsequent iteration, $d$ was pushed above $D_{\text{lower}}$, which deactivated the penalty completely ($\gamma = 0$). Without the penalty, $d$ dropped back below $D_{\text{lower}}$ by $6.986 \times 10^{-4}$, creating an exact, undamped 2-cycle periodic limit cycle ($+6.986 \times 10^{-4} \leftrightarrow -6.986 \times 10^{-4}$) that exhausted Abaqus cutbacks.
