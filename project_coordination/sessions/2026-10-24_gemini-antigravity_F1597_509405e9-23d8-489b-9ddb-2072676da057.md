## 2. Weak Form & Term-by-Term Energy Formulations

### A. Dual-Element Weak Form & Active DOFs
- **Phase-Field Subproblem (Layer 1: `JTYPE=1` quad / `JTYPE=3` tri, Active DOF 3)**:
  $$\int_{\Omega} \left[ \left(\frac{G_c}{l_0} + 2\mathcal{H}\right) d \delta d + G_c l_0 \nabla d \cdot \nabla \delta d \right] \mathrm{d}\Omega = \int_{\Omega} 2\mathcal{H} \delta d \,\mathrm{d}\Omega$$
  Element fracture surface energy:
  $$E_{\mathrm{frac}}^{(e)} = \sum_{p=1}^{N_{\mathrm{int}}} w_p \det(\mathbf{J}_p) B \cdot G_c \left[ \frac{\bar{d}_e^2}{2 l_0} + \frac{l_0}{2} |\nabla d(\boldsymbol{\xi}_p)|^2 \right]$$
  Routed in UEL: `ENERGY(7) = E_FRAC_ELEM`, `SVARS(17) = E_FRAC_ELEM`, `SV_E_FRAC(PHYSIDX) = E_FRAC_ELEM`.
- **Mechanical Subproblem (Layer 2: `JTYPE=2` quad / `JTYPE=4` tri, Active DOFs 1, 2)**:
  $$\int_{\Omega} \boldsymbol{\sigma} : \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}(\delta\mathbf{u}) \,\mathrm{d}\Omega = \mathbf{F}_{\mathrm{ext}}$$
  where $\boldsymbol{\sigma} = \left[(1-\bar{d}_e)^2 + k_{\mathrm{res}}\right] \mathbb{C}_0 : \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$.
  Element elastic strain energy:
  $$E_{\mathrm{elas}}^{(e)} = \sum_{p=1}^{N_{\mathrm{int}}} w_p \det(\mathbf{J}_p) B \cdot \left[ \frac{1}{2} \boldsymbol{\sigma}_p : \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_p \right]$$
  Routed in UEL: `ENERGY(2) = E_ELAS_ELEM`, `SVARS(17) = E_ELAS_ELEM`, `SV_E_ELAS(PHYSIDX) = E_ELAS_ELEM`.
