$$\min_{\mathbf{d}} \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{d}^T \mathbf{A}_d \mathbf{d} - \mathbf{b}_d(\mathbf{u}_k, \mathbf{H}_0)^T \mathbf{d} \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbf{d} \ge \mathbf{d}_{88}$$
