## 2. AMRC Source Inspection & Algorithmic Analysis

Inspected open-source repository [AMRC](https://github.com/aaroshdahal/AMRC) (Upstream commit `71aaceac455cd3683682f61ae37f1b71d0cd5e6a`, MIT License, Copyright 2026 Aarosh Dahal, Abhinav Gupta, Aditya Kumar):

### Compatible Concepts Identified:
1. **Hierarchically Anchored Refinement (`refine_from_coarse`)**:
   - Refines from a coarse background mesh rather than repeatedly remeshing deformed/distorted meshes.
   - Uses `scipy.spatial.cKDTree` on active zone points (`extract_active_zone_points`) to bisect edges within a refinement radius.
2. **Transfer of Inequality Bounds**:
   - AMRC transfers the phase upper bound $z_{\text{ub}}$ (`z_ub = z_old`) across meshes to enforce damage irreversibility in PETSc SNES.
   - For our damage convention ($d = 1 - z$), this translates to a transferred lower bound:
     $$d_{\text{lb}}(\mathbf{x}) = \text{transfer}(d_{\text{old}}(\mathbf{x})) \implies d^*(\mathbf{x}) \ge d_{\text{lb}}(\mathbf{x})$$
3. **Mid-Stagger Re-equilibration Sequence**:
   - In `try_amr`: solve $\mathbf{u} \to$ test trigger $\to$ refine $\to$ transfer $\mathbf{u}, z \to$ apply BCs $\to$ apply bounds $\to$ solve $z \to$ check stagger convergence.

### Required Abaqus Adaptations & Incompatibilities:
1. **Phase Field Convention**: AMRC uses intactness $z \in [0, 1]$ ($z=1$ intact, $z=0$ broken); Abaqus uses damage $d \in [0, 1]$ ($d=0$ intact, $d=1$ broken). Relationship: $z = 1 - d$.
2. **Constitutive Model**: AMRC uses Drucker-Prager strength surface; our thesis framework uses Bourdin AT2 variational formulation with strain history field $H$.
3. **History Storage**: AMRC has no integration-point history; Abaqus UEL enforces irreversibility via Kuhn-Tucker complementarity using Gauss-point $H$ stored in `SVARS(9..12)` via `*INITIAL CONDITIONS, TYPE=SOLUTION`.
4. **Displacement Restoration**: In Abaqus static steps, `*INITIAL CONDITIONS, TYPE=DISPLACEMENT` is ignored (Section 34.2.1); requires a prescribed boundary step or kinematic offset driver.
