## 4. Scientific Discovery: Dual-Mechanism Discretization Defect
The evaluation reveals that the nonmatching state transfer breakdown on Candidate C2 is governed by two separate physical mechanisms:

   The crack-opening-compatible reconstruction operator successfully eliminates the artificial kinematic strain surge, slashing effective undamaged strain energy by 98.7% to 99.7% and achieving rapid, monotone 2-to-3 iteration PDAS convergence.
2. **Phase-Field Discrete Laplacian Diffusion (Governing Defect)**:
   Even with artificial strain surges suppressed, the equilibrated reaction force still exhibits a -77.1% to -88.7% deficit. Inspection reveals that the donor mesh was coarse ($h_{\text{donor}} = 0.005$ mm $\approx \ell/1.5$), under-resolving the continuous damage profile $e^{-|y|/\ell}$. When this under-resolved profile is transferred onto the fine C2 mesh ($h = 0.0010$ mm $\le \ell/3.75$), the fine-mesh discrete Laplacian operator $\ell^2 \nabla^2 d$ relaxes the non-equilibrium transferred profile, spreading damage outward and softening the structure.

**Conclusion**: Eliminating the reaction force deficit requires both the crack-opening-compatible operator AND a donor discretization that already resolves $\ell$ ($h \le \ell/3.75$), or a constrained profile-preserving projection operator.
