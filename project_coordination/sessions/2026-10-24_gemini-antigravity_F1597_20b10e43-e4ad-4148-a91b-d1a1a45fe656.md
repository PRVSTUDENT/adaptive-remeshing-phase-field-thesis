## 2. Clarification of the PDAS Lower-Bound Multiplier Sign

The bound-constrained phase-field subproblem for discrete AT2 minimization is:
$$\min_d \frac{1}{2} d^T K_d d - d^T F_d \quad \text{subject to } d \ge d_{\text{prev}} \text{ and } d \le 1$$

With lower-bound multiplier $\lambda^- \ge 0$, the Lagrangian is:
$$\mathcal{L}(d, \lambda^-) = \frac{1}{2} d^T K_d d - d^T F_d - (\lambda^-)^T (d - d_{\text{prev}})$$

Stationarity $\nabla_d \mathcal{L} = 0$ requires:
$$K_d d - F_d - \lambda^- = 0 \implies \lambda^- = K_d d - F_d$$

In Abaqus convention, the internal residual vector passed by `f42_mixed_uel.for` is:
$$\text{RHS} = -(K_d d - F_d)$$

At any node where degree of freedom 3 is constrained by `*BOUNDARY`, Abaqus equilibrium yields:
$$R_3 + \text{RF3} = 0 \implies \text{RF3} = -R_3 = K_d d - F_d$$

Therefore:
$$\lambda^- = \text{RF3}$$

The physical and mathematical interpretation is rigorous:
- **Dual Feasibility ($\lambda^- \ge 0 \iff \text{RF3} \ge 0$)**: The internal phase-field resistance $(K_d d)_i$ exceeds the crack driving force $(F_d)_i$. A positive reaction $\text{RF3} \ge 0$ holds damage from healing below $d_{\text{prev}}$. The node is legitimately active.
- **Release Criterion ($\lambda^- < -\tau_{\text{res}} \iff \text{RF3} < -\tau_{\text{res}}$)**: The driving force $(F_d)_i$ exceeds resistance $(K_d d)_i$, indicating the crack tip wants to advance ($d > d_{\text{prev}}$). The node must be released into the free set.
- **Activation Criterion ($d_j < d_{\text{prev}, j} - \tau_{\text{primal}}$)**: A free node violates the irreversibility bound (healing detected). The node must be added to the active set.
