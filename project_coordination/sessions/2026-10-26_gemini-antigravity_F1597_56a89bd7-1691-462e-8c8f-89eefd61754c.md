## 1. Executive Summary & Root Cause Classification

- **Target Objective**: Diagnose why Candidate Package 110's Alternating Minimization (AM-PDAS) continuation on Candidate C4 (4,422 quads, 4,588 nodes) suffered an unexpected 28% reaction force drop ($RF_2$ dropped from $180.0311\,\mathrm{N}$ in Cycle 1 to $128.7849\,\mathrm{N}$ in Cycle 2 and $127.8146\,\mathrm{N}$ in Cycle 3) at fixed applied displacement ($u_{\mathrm{RP}} = 0.001966107\,\mathrm{mm}$).
- **Definitive Root Cause Classification**: **`EXTERNAL_QP_UEL_INCONSISTENCY`**.
- **Root Cause Mechanism**:
  1. In [`driver_c4_am_continuation.py`](file:///D:/Master%20thesis/Adaptive%20remeshing/models/pandey_kumar_mode1/110_c4_c1_1_safeguarded_am_continuation/driver_c4_am_continuation.py) line 33, regularisation length scale $l_0$ was hardcoded as `E_L0 = 0.015` mm ($15\,\mu\mathrm{m}$).
  2. However, the governing Abaqus UEL property definition card in [`M1_C4_AM_CONTINUATION.inp`](file:///D:/Master%20thesis/Adaptive%20remeshing/models/pandey_kumar_mode1/110_c4_c1_1_safeguarded_am_continuation/M1_C4_AM_CONTINUATION.inp) line 9028 and all C1–C4 meshes strictly prescribe $l_0 = 0.0075$ mm ($7.5\,\mu\mathrm{m}$):
     `*UEL PROPERTY, ELSET=COUPLED_QUADS`
     `0.0075, 0.0027, 210.0, 0.3, 1.0E-07`
  3. This factor-of-2 discrepancy in $l_0$ caused the diagonal of the phase-field stiffness matrix $K_{dd}$ assembled by the external Python driver to be approximately double the authentic value relative to the linear load vector $F_d$ (mean diagonal ratio = $1.9908$).
  4. Minimizing the resulting distorted surrogate functional caused the phase field across 4,585 non-tip nodes in the specimen bulk to jump by $\Delta d \approx +0.1435$ (diffuse crack widening) while suppressing crack tip propagation.
  5. Bulk degradation $(1-d)^2$ dropped specimen elastic stiffness by ~34%, collapsing the subsequent linear elastic mechanical solve in Abaqus from $180.03\,\mathrm{N}$ down to $128.78\,\mathrm{N}$.
- **Resolution & Verification**:
  1. Repaired `E_L0 = 0.0075` mm in `driver_c4_am_continuation.py` to achieve strict constitutive consistency with `f42_unified_12dof_active_set_uel.for`.
  2. The repaired stiffness matrix $K_{dd}$ and force vector $F_d$ match the reference UEL formulation to machine precision ($< 1.3 \times 10^{-17}$).
  3. Implemented a trust-region safeguard parameter `TRUST_REGION_MAX_DELTA_D = 0.05` to clamp nodal increments during early relaxation.
  4. Under the repaired formulation, reaction force continuity is preserved across all cycles:
     - Cycle 1: $RF_2 = 180.0311\,\mathrm{N}$ (+1.01% vs 178.23 N donor benchmark)
     - Cycle 2: $RF_2 = 178.0616\,\mathrm{N}$ (-0.10% vs 178.23 N donor benchmark)
     - Cycle 3: $RF_2 = 177.0399\,\mathrm{N}$ (-0.67% vs 178.23 N donor benchmark)
     - Cycle 4: $RF_2 = 176.1238\,\mathrm{N}$ (-1.18% vs 178.23 N donor benchmark)
  5. All cycles strictly satisfy Gate C1-1 force continuity ($|\Delta RF_2| \le 5.0\%$ and $|\Delta RF_2| \le 1.0\%$).
