## 4. Deep Root-Cause Analysis: Fixed-Point Operator Mechanics

### 4.1 Subproblem Stationarity vs. Coupled Fixed-Point Contraction
The diagnostic resolves the apparent paradox of why small phase residuals coexist with $\|\Delta d\|_\infty \approx 0.00145$:
1. **Conditional Phase Subproblem**: Given fixed displacement $u_k$, the Python PDAS solver solves:
   $$\min_{d \ge d_{\text{lower}}} \frac{1}{2} d^T K(u_k) d - q(u_k)^T d$$
   The solver satisfies the Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions to tolerance $10^{-9}\,\text{kN/mm}$. Hence, $d_k$ is the exact conditionally optimal damage for displacement $u_k$.
2. **Conditional Mechanical Subproblem**: Given prescribed damage $d_k$, Abaqus Newton-Raphson satisfies global equilibrium $\nabla_u \Pi(u, d_k) = 0$ to tolerance ($|\Delta \mathrm{RF2}| \le 0.09\,\text{N}$). Hence, $u_{k+1}$ is the exact conditionally optimal displacement for damage $d_k$.
3. **Coupled Fixed-Point Map**: Alternating minimization executes the composite Picard fixed-point map:
   $$d_{k+1} = \mathcal{T}(d_k) \equiv \text{PhaseSolve}(\text{MechSolve}(d_k))$$
   The residual $\|d_{k+1} - d_k\|_\infty = \|\mathcal{T}(d_k) - d_k\|_\infty$ measures **coupled fixed-point stationarity**, which is entirely independent of whether each subproblem was solved to machine precision.

### 4.2 Why Step Reduction Fails to Contract the Crack-Tip Mode
- Reducing macroscopic displacement increment $\Delta u$ from $17.8\,\text{nm}$ to $8.9\,\text{nm}$ and $4.45\,\text{nm}$ reduces external mechanical work $W_{\mathrm{ext}}$ ($0.00326\,\text{mJ} \to 0.00163\,\text{mJ} \to 0.00081\,\text{mJ}$).
- However, once the crack-tip node reaches $d \approx 0.65 - 0.70$, the local energetic driving force is dominated by the autonomous release of strain energy stored in the surrounding elastic bulk.
- The spectral radius $\rho(\mathcal{T}')$ of the coupled Jacobian at this softening state is governed by local material degradation:
  $$g'(d) = -2(1-d) < 0$$
  which creates positive feedback between local compliance and strain concentration.
- Because $\rho(\mathcal{T}') \to 0.999$, the fixed-point iteration crawls along the crack propagation manifold with an update of $\approx 0.00145$ per cycle, invariant to $\Delta u$.
