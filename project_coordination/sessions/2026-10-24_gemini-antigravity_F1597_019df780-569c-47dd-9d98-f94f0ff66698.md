## 5. Deconstruction of the Over-Softening Mechanism

Forward continuation simulations were performed using `scripts/validation/test_aligned_forward_continuation.py` to test whether enforcing $d \ge d_{H1}$ rather than $d \ge d_{\mathrm{ckpt}}$ prevents the tearing cascade:

- **Hypothesis A ($d \ge d_{\mathrm{ckpt}}$, unconstrained wake)**:
  Failed to converge in 15 staggered iterations ($\|\Delta d\|_{\infty} = 0.0217$).
  Reaction force dropped from $113.00\text{ N}$ to $73.28\text{ N}$ ($-39.72\text{ N}$, $-35.15\%$).
  Fracture energy surged from $0.1367\text{ mJ}$ to $0.1754\text{ mJ}$ ($+28.32\%$).
- **Hypothesis B ($d \ge d_{H1}$, checkpoint-preserving lower bound)**:
  Failed to converge in 15 staggered iterations ($\|\Delta d\|_{\infty} = 0.0216$).
  Reaction force dropped from $113.00\text{ N}$ to $73.29\text{ N}$ ($-39.71\text{ N}$, $-35.14\%$).
  Fracture energy surged from $0.1367\text{ mJ}$ to $0.1755\text{ mJ}$ ($+28.45\%$).

**The Underlying Cause Identified**:
In C4 (Package 94 Step 4), Abaqus did not solve the phase field on the entire mesh. Instead, **3,845 out of 4,588 nodes (83.8%) were held by Dirichlet boundary conditions (`*Boundary`)**, restricting the active phase solve to a tight crack-tip corridor:
$$X \in [0.0350, 0.0725]\text{ mm}, \quad Y \in [0.0424, 0.0576]\text{ mm}$$
When alternating minimization is performed globally across all 4,610 nodes without active-set isolation, the diffuse damage flanks in the wake undergo unconstrained local energy dissipation, causing an artificial loss of overall specimen stiffness.
