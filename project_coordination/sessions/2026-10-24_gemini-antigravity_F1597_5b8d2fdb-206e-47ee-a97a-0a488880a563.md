## 3. Root Cause Diagnosis: Intra-Increment Iteration History Ratcheting

A profound physical and numerical divergence between the offline Python reference and the Abaqus UEL implementation was identified:

1. **In the Offline Staggered Solver (Turn 107)**:
   - Alternating minimization solves the displacement field $\mathbf{u}$ with fixed damage $d$.
   - History $H$ is updated **only once per outer staggered iteration** using the converged displacement field:
     $$H_{\text{trial}} = \max(H_{\text{base}}, \psi_0^+(\mathbf{u}))$$
   - The damage step is then solved with fixed $\mathbf{u}$ under bound constraints $d \ge d_{\text{base}}$.
   - This scheme is unconditionally stable and energy-dissipative; intermediate trial overshoots cannot corrupt the history variable.

2. **In the Abaqus Monolithic UEL (`f42_experimental_irreversible_uel.for`)**:
   - Both phase-field (JTYPE 1) and mechanical (JTYPE 2) elements execute simultaneously in each Newton-Raphson iteration.
   - In JTYPE 2, `SV_H_TRIAL(PHYSIDX, KPT)` is updated during **every internal equilibrium iteration**:
     ```fortran
     IF (POS_M .GT. HIST) THEN
       HIST = POS_M
       SV_H_TRIAL(PHYSIDX, KPT) = POS_M
     ENDIF
     ```
   - In Newton-Raphson iterations under displacement control, intermediate trial displacement corrections frequently exhibit localized spatial overshoots.
   - Because `UEXTERNALDB(LOP=1)` is called only at the *start* of an increment, any iteration overshoot within an increment **permanently ratchets `SV_H_TRIAL` upward**.
   - In the next iteration of the same increment, JTYPE 1 sees this inflated driving force $H$, which forces damage $d$ higher.
   - Higher damage degrades stiffness $(1-d)^2$, causing even higher localized strains in subsequent iterations and increments.
   - Over 200 increments, this positive feedback loop drove catastrophic crack propagation across the specimen at a total displacement of only +5 nm.

3. **Proposed Numerical Hypothesis / Correction for Tomorrow**:
   - Decouple intra-increment iteration updates: freeze $H$ during Newton-Raphson equilibrium iterations and update `SV_H_TRIAL` only from the converged equilibrium state at `LOP=2` (or introduce a staggered iteration relaxation/damping parameter in UEXTERNALDB).
   - Test this hypothesis in a small offline single-element / patch test before launching a full cluster solve.
