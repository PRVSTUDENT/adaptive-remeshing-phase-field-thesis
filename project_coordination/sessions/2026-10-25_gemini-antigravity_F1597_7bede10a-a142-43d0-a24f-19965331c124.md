## 2. Abaqus Staggered Alternating Minimization Architecture

In Turn 119, monolithic block-Jacobi Picard iterations cut back in forward continuation because off-diagonal Jacobian blocks $\mathbf{K}_{ud} = \mathbf{K}_{du} = \mathbf{0}$ induced chattering between degraded stiffness and tensile strain spikes. In this turn, we implemented and demonstrated **genuine alternating minimization (AM)**:

1. **Subsolve 1: Mechanical Subproblem ($\min_{\mathbf{u}} \Pi_{\text{mech}}(\mathbf{u}; \mathbf{d}^{(k)})$)**:
   - Phase field $\mathbf{d}$ is held strictly fixed at previous accepted iterate $\mathbf{d}^{(k)}$.
   - Solves linear elasticity: $\mathbf{K}_{uu}(\mathbf{d}^{(k)}) \mathbf{u}^{(k+1)} = \mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}}$.
   - Converges in **1 single iteration** with subsolve equilibrium residual $\|R_u\| < 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{kN} \le 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kN}$.
   - Evaluates undegraded positive strain energy density $\psi_0^+(\mathbf{u}^{(k+1)})$ at each Gauss point:
     $$\psi_0^+ = \frac{1}{2} C_{12} \langle \text{tr}(\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}) \rangle_+^2 + C_{33} (\varepsilon_{11}^2 + \varepsilon_{22}^2 + 2\varepsilon_{12}^2)$$
   - Updates candidate driving history using the Turn 119 non-ratcheting rule:
     $$H_{\text{cand}} = \max\left(H_{\text{committed}}, \psi_0^+(\mathbf{u}^{(k+1)})\right)$$

2. **Subsolve 2: Phase-Field Subproblem ($\min_{\mathbf{d}} \Pi_{\text{phase}}(\mathbf{d}; H_{\text{cand}})$ subject to $\mathbf{d} \ge \mathbf{d}_{\text{transferred}}$)**:
   - Mechanical displacement $\mathbf{u}$ is held strictly fixed at $\mathbf{u}^{(k+1)}$ (fixing driving force $2 H_{\text{cand}}$).
   - Solves bound-constrained quadratic variational inequality with active-set penalty $\gamma (\mathbf{d}_{\text{lower}} - \mathbf{d})$.
   - Converges in **3 Newton iterations** with free-phase residual $\|R_d\| < 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{kN/mm} \le 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{kN/mm}$.
   - Dual multiplier on active nodes: $\lambda_i^- = (\mathbf{K}_{\text{pure}} \mathbf{d} - \mathbf{F}_{\text{pure}})_i \ge 0$.

3. **Distinction between Individual Outer Cycle and Full AM Convergence**:
   - **Individual Outer Cycle (Cycle 1)**: Both Subsolves 1 and 2 solve to machine precision ($R_{u, \text{sub}} \le 10^{-14}$, $R_{d, \text{sub}} \le 10^{-12}$), but the coupled residual $R_{u, \text{coupled}} = \|F_{\text{ext}} - \mathbf{K}_{uu}(\mathbf{d}^{(1)}) \mathbf{u}^{(1)}\|$ reflects the stiffness change $\Delta \mathbf{K}(\mathbf{d}^{(1)} - \mathbf{d}^{(0)})$.
   - **Full Alternating Minimization Process**: Outer iterations are repeated ($\mathbf{u} \to \mathbf{d} \to \mathbf{u} \to \mathbf{d}$) until both $\|\mathbf{d}^{(k+1)} - \mathbf{d}^{(k)}\| \le 10^{-6}$ and $\|R_{u, \text{coupled}}\| \le 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{kN}$.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Abaqus State-Preservation Findings

A rigorous keyword-level audit established the exact mechanisms governing Abaqus UEL state preservation:
1. **`*INITIAL CONDITIONS, TYPE=DISPLACEMENT` (UNSUPPORTED IN STATIC ANALYSIS)**:
   * Confirmed via Abaqus Analysis User's Guide Section 34.2.1: initial displacements are completely ignored in static analysis.
   * If an external Python driver spawns a *new* static job without carrying over displacements, Abaqus resets nodal coordinates to zero. Displacements must either be held in-memory across sequential steps or explicitly enforced via `*BOUNDARY` on mechanical DOFs during the phase solve.
2. **`*INITIAL CONDITIONS, TYPE=SOLUTION` (SUPPORTED)**:
   * Safely populates `SVARS(9..12)` for integration-point history $H$.
3. **`*BOUNDARY, OP=NEW` (TEMPORARY CONSTRAINT RELEASE)**:
   * In sequential multi-step solves, `*BOUNDARY, OP=NEW` cleanly releases temporary subproblem constraints, preventing artificial crack pinning.
4. **Turn 119 Non-Ratcheting History Invariant**:
   * Evaluated as $H_{\text{cand}} = \max(H_{\text{committed}}, \psi_0^+(u))$, eliminating intra-increment trial overshoots and wake tearing.
