## 3. Mathematical & Dimensional Verification of Canonical PDAS Active Set

### 3.1 Governing Kuhn–Tucker Complementarity
The discrete phase-field subproblem under irreversibility $d \ge d_0$ satisfies:
$$K_d d - f_d - \lambda = 0, \quad \lambda \ge 0, \quad d - d_0 \ge 0, \quad \lambda_i (d_i - d_{0,i}) = 0$$
where $\lambda_i = r_i = (K_d d - f_d)_i$ is the dual multiplier representing residual crack driving energy in $[\text{kN mm}]$.

### 3.2 Canonical Active-Set Update
The Hintermüller–Ito–Kunisch criterion partitions the nodal domain into:
- **Active set:** $\mathcal{A}_k = \{i : d_i^{(k)} - c \lambda_i^{(k)} \le d_{0,i} + \epsilon_{\text{PDAS}}\}$ (nodes where crack growth is inactive; damage clamped to $d_0$ to prevent healing).
- **Free set:** $\mathcal{I}_k = \{i : d_i^{(k)} - c \lambda_i^{(k)} > d_{0,i} + \epsilon_{\text{PDAS}}\}$ (nodes where crack driving force exceeds resistance; unconstrained damage evolution).

### 3.3 Dimensional Harmonization
To ensure dimensional homogeneity, $c$ must scale $[\text{kN mm}]$ to dimensionless damage:
- Chosen characteristic penalty: $c = 1.0 \times 10^3\text{ (kN mm)}^{-1}$.
- Dimensionless damage tolerance: $\epsilon_{\text{PDAS}} = 1.0 \times 10^{-6}$.
- At the transfer initial condition where $d \equiv d_0$, the criterion becomes:
$$-c r_i \le \epsilon_{\text{PDAS}} \iff r_i \ge -\frac{\epsilon_{\text{PDAS}}}{c} = -1.0 \times 10^{-9}\text{ kN mm}$$
