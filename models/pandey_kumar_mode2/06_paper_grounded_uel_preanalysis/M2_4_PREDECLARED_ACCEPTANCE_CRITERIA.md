## 2. Epistemic Classification Rules

1. **Category `PAPER_VERIFIED`:**
   - Applied only to parameters explicitly printed in Section 4.2 text: domain dimensions ($1 \times 1\,\text{mm}$), initial crack length ($a_0 = 0.5\,\text{mm}$), Young's modulus ($E=210\,\text{GPa}$), Poisson's ratio ($\nu=0.3$), critical fracture energy ($G_c=2.7\,\text{N/mm}$), length scale ($l_0=15\,\mu\text{m}$), boundary conditions (fixed bottom, top shear displacement control), and constitutive split (Miehe spectral split).
2. **Category `UNRESOLVED`:**
   - The exact numerical value of `errorTarget` used by Pandey & Kumar for Mode-II is **not stated in the paper text** and remains strictly classified as `UNRESOLVED`.
3. **Category `INFERRED / PROJECT_SELECTED_FOR_M2_4`:**
   - Candidate `ET_2PCT` ($22{,}530$ elements) was selected based on non-targeted discretization metrics: Pearson $r = -0.8202$, $98.65\%$ top-10% error focus, continuous crack corridor, $h_{\min} = 0.73\,\mu\text{m} \le l_0/2$, and feasible model size ($N \le 40{,}000$).
4. **Post-Hoc Reproduction Sanity Checks:**
   - The published mesh count ($19{,}963$ elements) and published figures (Fig. 6b, Fig. 12, Fig. 13a) serve strictly as external post-hoc benchmarks to evaluate physical fidelity, not as tuning targets.
